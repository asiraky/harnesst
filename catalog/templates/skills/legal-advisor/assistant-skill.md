# Legal Advisor (installed skill)

Teaches the agent to draft privacy policies, terms of service, cookie policies, DPAs, and
disclaimers — GDPR/CCPA/LGPD-aware, jurisdiction-specific, with mandatory disclosures and a
compliance checklist. Knowledge only: no tools, wakes, or human-contact behaviour. The skill's
files are template-owned — report a broken template to an administrator instead of hand-fixing.
